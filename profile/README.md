## Digi Petroleum

**Petroleum services.** Production engineering, surface network modelling and simulation, flow assurance, and digital tools that turn field data into engineering decisions.

### What we do

- **Production engineering**: well and facility performance, from the reservoir to the separator.
- **Surface network modelling and simulation**: gathering networks, flowlines and surface equipment, solved and calibrated against field measurements.
- **Flow assurance**: pressure, temperature, velocity and erosion screening across the network.
- **Digital oilfield tools**: engineering software built around how field data is collected and used.
